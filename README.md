# GeoNiti

### Digital Land Acquisition & Geospatial Project Management Platform

GeoNiti is a digital platform designed to **simplify, track, and manage the land acquisition process** from initial proposal to completion.

It brings land records, project progress, geospatial information, approvals, compensation, documents, and field activities into a **single centralized platform**.

---

## Why GeoNiti?

Land acquisition involves multiple stakeholders, departments, approvals, documents, and field activities. When these processes are managed through disconnected systems or physical records, it becomes difficult to:

* Know the current status of a project
* Track land and affected parcels
* Monitor pending approvals
* Manage compensation
* Locate projects geographically
* Maintain a clear history of decisions and actions
* Identify delays and bottlenecks

**GeoNiti addresses this by creating a single digital view of the entire acquisition lifecycle.**

---

## What GeoNiti Does

### Visualize Land & Projects

View projects and land parcels on an interactive map, making it easier to understand **where acquisition activities are happening and what is happening at each location**.

### Manage the Acquisition Process

Track a project through its different stages — from proposal and approvals to field verification, compensation, and completion.

### Centralize Documents & Records

Maintain project-related documents and records digitally instead of relying on scattered physical files and disconnected information.

### Track Compensation

Monitor compensation assessment and disbursement to understand what has been completed and what remains pending.

### Monitor Progress

Dashboards provide an overall view of project progress, acquisition targets, compensation, and delays.

### Keep Stakeholders Updated

Notifications and workflow updates help relevant stakeholders stay informed when important actions or project statuses change.

### Maintain Accountability

Project actions and status changes are recorded, creating a transparent history of activities and decisions.

---

## How It Works

GeoNiti connects the major parts of the land acquisition lifecycle:

```text
Project Proposal
       ↓
   Approvals
       ↓
Land & Parcel Identification
       ↓
  Field Verification
       ↓
Compensation
       ↓
Project Completion
```

Instead of managing each stage separately, GeoNiti provides a **single system to track the complete journey**.

## Technology

GeoNiti is built as a modern web platform using:

* **React** — User interface
* **Node.js & Express** — Backend services
* **PostgreSQL + PostGIS** — Data and geospatial information
* **Leaflet** — Interactive mapping
* **Docker** — Application deployment
